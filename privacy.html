Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12-08-2026

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Struyach Fitness Apps collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users (“User”) of the Struyach Fitness Apps mobile applications (“App”), available on both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We may collect the following personal information from Users:

  • Email and password: We require Users to create an account using their email and password to access certain features of the App.
  • Avatar images: The App allows Users to upload and store avatar images for their profiles.
  • Personal records in weightlifting and gymnastics: Users can input and store personal weightlifting records within the App.
  • Liked posts and workouts: The App may collect information about posts and workouts that Users have liked.
  • Comments and media: The App collects and stores any comments made by Users, including text, video, and photo media.
  • In-app purchases and subscriptions: The App uses RevenueCat for in-app purchases. RevenueCat tracks and records Users’ in-app purchases and subscription details and collects purchase history from Users. This data is used to evaluate user behaviour, understand the effectiveness of existing product features, plan new features, or measure the audience size or characteristics. RevenueCat also collects a user ID to authenticate Users, enable features, prevent fraud, implement security measures, ensure server uptime, minimize app crashes, improve scalability and performance, or perform customer support.

1.2 Data Storage

All User data mentioned above is securely stored on servers hosted by Firebase and RevenueCat. We take appropriate measures to protect the data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Use of Collected Information

2.1 Personalization and Service Improvement

The collected information is used to provide personalized experiences, improve our services, and optimize the App’s performance. This includes, but is not limited to, suggesting relevant content, tracking workout progress, and enhancing user interaction within the App.

2.2 Notification Management

Users can manage their notification preferences within the App’s settings.

2.3 Email Privacy

Users have the option to hide their email from other Users within the App.

2.4 Subscriptions Management

Users can manage their subscriptions through the device settings. Additional terms regarding subscriptions are outlined in the Paywalls Terms of Use.

Data Sharing

3.1 Third-Party Sharing

We do not share User data with third parties in any way, except as necessary to provide the App’s services and as required by law enforcement agencies or applicable regulations.

Account Deletion

4.1 Account Deactivation

Users can choose to permanently delete their accounts and all associated data from the App. This action is irreversible and cannot be undone.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. If there are any changes to our Privacy Policy, Users will be shown a new version, and they must agree to it in order to continue using the App.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the App, please contact us at feedback.struyach@gmail.com.
